Create one original Salvador Dalí-inspired surreal oil painting in the precise illusionistic language of his 1930s ambiguous images. Landscape 3:2, full artwork with no lettering, signature, border or frame. Theme: a room remembers the person who lived there.

Depict a quiet, empty old coastal room, seen nearly front-on but with clear depth across its worn stone floor. Its architecture and curtains form a single large, subtly legible human face using shared physical shapes. It must read both as an actual interior and as a face occupying most of the image, with no separate painted portrait and no superimposed transparent face. The two readings occupy exactly the same space.

Geometric mapping: two dark, narrow arched window recesses across the back wall become the two eyes; short faded curtain folds over them serve as brows. The warm narrow plaster pier between the windows, lit on the left and shadowed on the right, reads as the bridge and lower edge of a nose. Below, a gently curved shallow stone step has a thin dark seam that reads as closed lips. The pale rounded edge of that same step reads as the chin. Soft long curtains at the far left and right frame the wall and define the cheeks and head contour. Every facial feature is made entirely from these architectural contours, cast shadows and drapery; the room's materials and walkable floor remain continuous. Keep the facial geometry restrained and close enough to human proportions that the second reading appears at a glance, while closer inspection resolves back into room details. Do not add literal skin, eyeballs, a head sculpture, or a face-shaped hole.

A small unoccupied wooden stool at the left foreground establishes room scale. Through the recesses, a sliver of pale blue sea is visible. Low afternoon light enters from the left; warm bone plaster, weathered muted terracotta curtain folds, umber recesses and blue-green distance share a quiet palette. Use finely layered oil paint, convincing stone and fabric, selective clear edges and continuous tonal modeling. The image should feel still, psychologically unsettling, and patiently painted, rather than a glossy architectural render. The central achievement is that the SAME shapes sustain both readings.
