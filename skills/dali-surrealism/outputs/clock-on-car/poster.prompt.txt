A Salvador Dalí 1930s hallucinatory oil painting, Persistence of Memory language applied to a new subject. Precisely drawn transformed silhouettes, smooth sculptural tonal modeling, broad quiet painted planes.

A 1930s dark automobile sits on a vast dry plain. A large soft pocket watch with a clearly readable pale dial, numerals and hands is draped over the hood and front fender. The watch body has lost rigidity: it folds with continuous thickness, wrinkles, gravity and a contact shadow, yet remains a watch. The car stays a car—headlamps, grille, running boards, spoked wheels—metal yielding only where the watch weights it.

Low horizon, ochre-and-bone ground, sky cooling to blue-grey. Raking light from the left; one long simple shadow of car and draped clock. Fine detail at the watch hands, numerals, and a chrome headlamp; the plain and sky stay quiet painted planes.

Colors: ochre ground, bone-white dial, blue-grey distance, deep brown shadows, dark olive-black car body, a small warm gold in the watch case. No extra title text, no signature, no extra faces.
