A Salvador Dalí 1930s hallucinatory oil painting of Beijing in autumn, with precisely drawn transformed silhouettes, smooth sculptural tonal modeling, and broad quiet painted planes. No text, no signature, no watermark.

In the left-center foreground a colossal fan-shaped ginkgo leaf stands as a sculptural landform. Its upper outer contour continuously becomes the upturned eave of a Forbidden City palace hall: the same curve reads first as a ginkgo fan, then as a Chinese roof eave. Imperial yellow glazed barrel tiles and circular drip-end tiles keep their ceramic identity but have lost rigidity; the roof drapes over the leaf’s edge with continuous thickness, gravity folds, and weight, sagging toward the beaten-earth courtyard. The gold of the ginkgo and the gold of the glazed tiles share one continuous light and color so the metamorphosis stays readable. A pocket watch with a clear clock face and hands drapes over this hybrid eave, folding with leather-and-metal thickness where it hangs. A thin forked wooden crutch actually bears the heaviest sagging corner of the tiled eave at a tiny contact point.

Composition is a 3:2 landscape, subject off-center left, with large empty space. A long vermillion palace wall stretches unnaturally far to the right across a pale ochre and bone-white northern Chinese courtyard, receding to a low horizon. Far in the distance a small, precise Forbidden City corner tower with stacked multi-eaves sits under a clear, cold Beijing autumn sky. A few fallen ginkgo fans lie on the ground; one ripe persimmon hangs from a thin bare branch near the wall as a small concentrated red. No people.

Clear dry afternoon light from the left; long, simple painted shadows. Working colors: large ochre-and-bone courtyard; pale cool Beijing blue sky with slow internal gradients; shared ginkgo-gold and imperial yellow on the hybrid form (sunlit gold, half-tone ochre, shadow burnt umber); vermillion wall as secondary mass; deepest darks in Prussian-blue-black under the draping roof, clock, and wall shadow; a small persimmon orange-red accent. Finest drawing at the leaf-to-eave junction, tile folds, clock face, and crutch contact; sky, ground, and distant wall kept as quiet painted planes.
