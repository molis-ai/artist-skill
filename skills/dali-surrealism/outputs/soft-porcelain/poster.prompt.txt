Create an original surreal oil painting in the visual language of Salvador Dalí's 1930s paintings. Landscape 3:2. Theme: the fatigue that a resting object cannot put down. Build the image through precisely drawn transformed silhouettes, smooth sculptural tonal modeling, broad quiet painted planes and a few exact details. Fine, thin oil-color handling; the painting's design is visible in the selection of shapes and tones.

A single ivory teacup is becoming an elongated soft organic form, draped from a low rectangular ochre ledge. Its oval opening and one loop handle identify the cup, while the lower wall stretches into a long tapering fold that has lost the ordinary cup's proportions. One slender forked wooden prop touches this heavy drooping fold. The supported point makes a tiny crease. The narrow handle echoes the drooping outline in a sinuous loop. Draw these few contours with patient precision. Give the cup a broad bone-white lit plane, a quiet ochre half-tone and a smooth cool blue-grey turning shadow, with one small reserved light accent on the rim. Let the volume and folded thickness be clear. The surfaces within these shapes remain calm and largely untextured.

Place this form across the left middle of the painting, leaving a large uninterrupted expanse on the right. A high, remote horizon separates a narrow still turquoise sea from a gradually changing blue and pale yellow sky. A small angular golden cliff near the far right horizon answers the cup. The dry plain recedes through broad continuous ochre tones, with very sparse descriptive marks. A long, sharply composed dark umber cast-shadow shape extends diagonally across the empty ground. The ledge is described with a clear top plane and dark side plane, plus only one or two drawn fissures. Everything participates in the same restrained painted organization.

Concentrate detail on the rim contour, the fold's turn, the slender handle and the tiny support contact. Elsewhere, choose large color masses and slowly modulated tones. The result should have the stillness of a lucid dream and the unnatural elegance of a deliberately transformed shape. Keep brushwork unobtrusive and the image optically clear. Full artwork, no text, signature, frame, border or artificial aged-canvas overlay.
