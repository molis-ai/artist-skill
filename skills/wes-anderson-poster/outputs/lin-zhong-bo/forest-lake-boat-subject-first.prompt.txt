Use case: ads-marketing.
Deliverable: one original Wes Anderson-style cinema poster, portrait 2:3, exact title "林中泊".

Reference role: the attached poster collection is a composition and hierarchy reference. Learn how a clearly authored subject or group is presented against subordinate scenery, with retro art direction and integrated title design. Do not copy any actor, movie title, hotel, credits, webpage text or card frame.

Focal subject: ONE complete vintage wooden rowing boat is the protagonist. Forest and lake are its setting, not equal competing focal subjects. Give the boat a distinctive, readable silhouette and enough visual presence to carry the poster, approximately 65–70% of poster width in the lower half, large enough to read as the unmistakable lead subject. Design it as a carefully presented physical object: matte coral-red painted planks, pale butter-cream gunwales, honey-colored timber seats, a coherent pair of real supported oars. Preserve real wood joints and interior depth.

Subject organization and style: arrange the boat and its existing oars as one deliberate visual unit. Present the boat broadside, almost parallel to the picture plane, with a slightly raised camera showing the real interior. Its long complete silhouette forms the main horizontal visual unit, like a carefully staged portrait of this object. Two oars lie neatly supported inside it in a deliberate parallel arrangement. Keep the boat mostly level, not dramatically diagonal. Give the boat fine, precise mid-century nautical design and poised retro character. Use spacing, proportions, physical color and precise presentation to create quiet, slightly eccentric formality. Do not require every part of the boat or environment to mirror.

Symmetry scope: the focal arrangement and the title/subject relationship may share a stable axis. DO NOT make the forest symmetric. No twin walls of trees, no tree tunnel, no giant foreground pine curtains and no arch around the boat.

Background: put the forest BEHIND the boat, across the far shore as a subordinate natural tree line with varying heights, gaps and overlapping foliage. The distant tree line runs across the BACK of the image around 55% of poster height, its foliage occupying a restrained background band. Its trees vary in height and shape but none reaches the top or foreground edges. Let a generous, nearly cloudless open sky occupy the upper part; use a sophisticated clear powder-blue, with a natural photographic light transition. Do not place any near tree at either edge. The lake supports the subject with a clear water plane and receding ripples. Use subject separation, controlled background detail and balanced areas to create poster hierarchy. Do not turn the composition into a sweeping landscape postcard.

Color design: the coral-red and butter-cream boat is the warm focal group; blue-green lake and dark olive/bottle-green distant forest provide cool support. Sky is a broad quieter powder-blue or pale warm-neutral area. The subject must stand out before the title is read. Vivid art direction with photographic light variation; no neon water, uniform teal wash or muddy sepia filter.

Layout: the boat's center is around 75% of poster height, with water below and a clean stretch of lake separating it from the far forest. The composition is a subject-led poster, not a window looking down a forest corridor. A calm, formal object portrait with an authored warm/cool palette, refined cinema-title craft and subtle tactile detail should evoke Wes Anderson before the title is read.

Camera and physical reality: photographed full-scale materials in an authored arrangement. Normal perspective, level camera, sufficient detail to read the boat's interior and curved outer hull. Warm diffused daylight from upper camera-left and cooler environmental fill; coherent lit and shaded planes, waterline, small contact ripples and a broken reflection. Background trees remain photographically credible while visually subordinate. No illustration, plastic pine models, flattened hull, sticker shadow or artificial global blur.

Typography: draw exactly "林中泊" once in a single title group in the upper calm region, integrated with the boat's overall placement. Approximately 40–55% of poster width and 8–12% of poster height. Original slender Chinese title lettering with open counters, moderate thick/thin contrast, gently curved tapered terminals and a coherent wordmark silhouette related to the rounded boat. All characters and radicals intact. Subtle tall stems with rounded tapered terminals and a gentle shared shallow arc, including a restrained sweep in the outside strokes of 林, echoed quietly in the other characters. Do not merely typeset a common Song/Ming font or use loose brush calligraphy. Deep oxblood-red flat printed ink; no generic ultra-bold block type, extrusion, shadow or glow.
No additional text, people, buildings, animals, extra boats, invented credits or decorative story props. One boat only.

Check the result in this order: a clear boat protagonist; authored subject arrangement and retro character; subordinate forest behind it; physical photographic depth and contact; scene color hierarchy; accurate crafted title. Symmetric scenery and a title do not compensate for an underdesigned subject.
